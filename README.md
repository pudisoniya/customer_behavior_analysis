# 🛍️ Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis
### Turning 3,900 Customer Purchases into Actionable Business Insights

**What makes this project interesting?** It goes beyond looking at sales numbers. It explores how customer segments, product preferences, discounts, subscriptions, and shipping choices reveal patterns that can support better business decisions.

## 📌 Project Snapshot

This project analyzes **3,900 customer purchases across 18 data columns** to understand shopping behavior, identify product trends, compare customer groups, and develop data-driven business recommendations.

## 🎯 Business Questions Explored

- Which customer groups contribute more to revenue?
- Which products receive the highest average ratings?
- How are customers distributed across loyalty segments?
- Which products show higher discount dependence?
- How do subscription and shipping choices relate to purchasing patterns?
- What opportunities can improve customer engagement and product positioning?

## ⚙️ Analytical Workflow

**01 — Data Exploration with Python**
- Inspected the dataset using `df.info()` and `df.describe()`.
- Explored customer demographics, purchase details, and shopping behavior.

**02 — Data Cleaning & Preparation**
- Identified 37 missing values in Review Rating.
- Imputed missing ratings using the median rating by product category.
- Created `age_group` and `purchase_frequency_days`.
- Standardized column names to snake_case.
- Dropped the `promo_code_used` column.

**03 — SQL-Based Analysis**
- Compared revenue generated by male and female customers.
- Analyzed product performance and customer purchasing patterns.

**04 — Dashboard & Visual Insights**
- Presented customer metrics, category revenue, and subscription distribution through dashboard visuals.

## 📊 Key Discoveries

| Analysis | Finding |
|---|---|
| Total customers | 3,900 |
| Average purchase amount | $59.76 |
| Average product rating | 3.75 |
| Highest-rated product | Gloves — 3.86 |
| Largest customer segment | Loyal — 3,116 |
| Standard shipping average | $58.46 |
| Express shipping average | $60.48 |
| Subscription split | 27% Yes / 73% No |

**Top-rated products:** Gloves (3.86), Sandals (3.84), Boots (3.82), Hat (3.80), and Skirt (3.78).

**Category revenue:** Clothing ($100K), Accessories ($75K), Footwear ($35K), and Outerwear ($20K).

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- **Subscription Growth:** Promote exclusive benefits to encourage more subscriptions.
- **Customer Retention:** Reward repeat buyers and strengthen loyalty programs.
- **Discount Optimization:** Review discount-dependent products while considering profitability.
- **Product Positioning:** Highlight highly rated products in marketing campaigns.
- **Targeted Marketing:** Focus on high-revenue age groups and express-shipping customers.
